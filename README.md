# Level Up Trader

A futures trading practice game. Trade a simulated market with realistic candles, trends, ranges,
breakouts and pullbacks. Clear levels, earn coins, use powerups, and review your stats.

No live data and no brokerage connection. Everything runs in your browser, and your progress is saved on your device.

**Play:** https://vantarisventures.github.io/Level-Up-Trader/

## Disclaimer

Level Up Trader is a simulation for education and entertainment only. The market ("EQ") is synthetic and prices are not real.
Nothing here is financial advice, and results in the game do not predict real trading results.
